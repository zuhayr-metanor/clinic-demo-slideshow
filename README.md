# Clinic Demo Slideshow

Metanor's introductory presentation for clinics, with the WhatsApp assistant and clinic website demos built into it.

**Live:** https://zuhayr-metanor.github.io/clinic-demo-slideshow/

## Presenting

- Arrow keys / click: next and previous slide
- **D**: jump to the live demo
- **N**: speaker notes
- **B**: blank screen
- **F**: fullscreen

Type the client's name on the first slide ("Prepared for"); it is remembered in that browser.

## Sources for the research slide

- About half of online doctor bookings are made 5 PM to 9 AM: [Zocdoc, When do patients want to see the doctor? (2023 data)](https://www.zocdoc.com/resources/blog/article/when-do-patients-want-to-see-the-doctor/)
- 91% (90.9%) of people in Saudi Arabia use WhatsApp: CST Saudi Internet Report 2023, as reported by [ArabiaWeather](https://arabiaweather.com/en/content/هذا-التطبيق-يستخدمه-90-من-سكان-السعودية-بحسب-تقرير-هيئة-الاتصالات)
- Replying within an hour makes firms nearly 7× as likely to reach the decision maker: Oldroyd, McElheran and Elkington, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads", Harvard Business Review, March 2011
- 23% average no-show rate across 105 studies: [Dantas et al., Health Policy, 2018](https://www.pubmed.gov/29482948/)
- Text reminders raise attendance by 14% (RR 1.14, 8 trials): [Gurol-Urganci et al., Cochrane Review, 2013](https://www.cochrane.org/CD007458)
